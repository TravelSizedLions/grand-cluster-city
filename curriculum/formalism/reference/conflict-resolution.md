# Notation Conflict Resolution

## What This Is
A disambiguation guide for mathematical symbols that mean different things in different fields. This helps you determine which interpretation of a symbol is correct based on context.

## When to Use This
- You recognize a symbol but it seems to mean something different than expected
- You're working across multiple mathematical fields and need to distinguish symbol meanings
- You're reading interdisciplinary research and encounter notation conflicts
- You need to understand why the same symbol is being used in seemingly contradictory ways

## How This Works
Problematic symbols are organized by the symbol itself, then broken down by field-specific meanings. Each entry provides:
- **Context clues**: How to tell which interpretation is intended
- **Field-specific definitions**: What the symbol means in each mathematical area
- **Disambiguation strategies**: Techniques for determining the correct interpretation
- **Common mixups**: Typical confusion points and how to avoid them

## Structure
Each entry follows this format:
```
## Symbol: [symbol]
**Why it's confusing**: [brief explanation of the conflict]

### Field 1: [field name]
- **Meaning**: [specific definition]
- **Context clues**: [how to identify this usage]
- **Example**: [typical appearance]

### Field 2: [field name]
- **Meaning**: [specific definition]
- **Context clues**: [how to identify this usage]
- **Example**: [typical appearance]

**Resolution strategy**: [how to distinguish between the different meanings]
```

## Usage Tips
- Look for the surrounding notation to determine context
- Pay attention to what other symbols appear alongside the problematic one
- Consider the academic field or paper source
- When in doubt, check the field-identification reference for broader context
