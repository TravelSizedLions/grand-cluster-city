### Exercise 2:
1. $3 \times x + y$
2. $3 \cdot x + y$
3. $3 * x + y$
4. $3x + y$
5. $f(x) = 3x + y$

### Exercise 3:
1. $8 \div 2 + 1 = 4 + 1 = 5$
2. $\frac{8}{2 + 1} = \frac{8}{3} \approx 2.67$
3. $\frac{8}{2} + 1 = 4 + 1 = 5$
4. $(8 \div 2) + 1 = 4 + 1 = 5$

Expressions 1, 3, and 4 are equivalent. Expression 2 is different due to parentheses.

### Exercise 4:
1. $2 + 3 \times 4 = 2 + 12 = 14$ (multiply first)
2. $(2 + 3) \times 4 = 5 \times 4 = 20$ (parentheses first)
3. $2 + 3 \times 4^2 = 2 + 3 \times 16 = 2 + 48 = 50$ (exponent, then multiply, then add)
4. $2[3 + 4(5 - 1)] = 2[3 + 4(4)] = 2[3 + 16] = 2[19] = 38$
